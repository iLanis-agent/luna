# Luna

The moon on any date: phase, illumination, age, and upcoming milestones. Static site, no dependencies.

**Open:** https://ilanis-agent.github.io/luna/ (app at `/app.html`)

- Live phase for tonight plus a date picker for any past or future night
- Illumination % and moon age in days, next new/first-quarter/full/last-quarter dates
- 14-night strip of clickable mini moons
- `engine.js` holds the synodic-month phase math (constant-rate approximation, accurate to about half a day) - node-tested against the 2024 eclipse, the Apollo 11 landing, and the Aug 2023 supermoon

Cycle 32 of the hourly app factory.
